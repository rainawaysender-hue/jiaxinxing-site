Context /  From isolated alerts to a governance system

National context / 2020

Bank accounts were a critical point in the movement of illicit funds.

China was facing a high level of telecom and online fraud. In 2019, authorities reported 200,000 telecom-fraud cases nationwide; in the first half of 2020, 101,000 cases were solved. National campaigns increasingly emphasized warning, interception, fund control, information sharing and technology-enabled prevention.

Regional implementation / Dalian

The operational problem was larger than identifying one fraudulent account.

The branch needed to connect external case lists with internal customer information, identify related accounts, recognize suspicious transaction patterns and translate findings into practical controls across onboarding and ongoing account use.

○ Behavioural model /  From transaction traces to risk features

Transaction behaviour was translated into observable features that could support daily review and more timely intervention.

Velocity
Fast in / fast out Short-cycle inflow and outflow.
Testing
Small-value probing Small transactions used to test account availability.
Dormancy
Reactivated accounts Inactive accounts suddenly returning to activity.
Pattern
Repeated equal-value transfers Multiple transfers approaching configured limits.
Timing
Late-night activity Unusual transaction timing as a contextual signal.
Counterparty
Remote personal accounts Cross-bank transfers to individuals in distant regions.

○ Risk engine /  Daily monitoring, grading and intervention

Customer volume, transaction activity, shareholder information and counterparties were used as risk dimensions. The project also pushed toward a daily, near-real-time monitoring model and further exploration of real-time transaction risk controls.

Low Normal monitoring
Watch Enhanced review
High Online limit adjustment
Critical Restriction / suspension

Customer & frontline controls /  Embedding risk into the workflow

Customer-facing

Make risk visible at the point of action.

  • Online and offline risk warnings
  • Additional checks for new accounts and account changes
  • Review of large-value and corporate-to-personal transfers
  • Corporate-to-personal transaction limits
Customer managers

Move controls closer to the customer.

  • On-site verification
  • Business background checks
  • Beneficial-owner and actual-controller identification
  • Review of abnormal transaction behaviour during the relationship
  • Clear division of responsibilities according to authority, customer knowledge and timeliness
