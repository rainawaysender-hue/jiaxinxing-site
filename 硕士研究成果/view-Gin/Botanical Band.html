01 / The Idea

The concept behind is to release gin in the same way as a music album.
Every batch is the product of a specific place, a specific time, and the local character of that place.

02 / Northeast
LAND / WINTER / RED

The depths of late autumn
The End of the Universe 。

Most plants have completed their growth cycle and are about to face a long winter. Amidst the bleakness, the red of the mountain hawthorn is the only sign of life remaining.

Leave the hawthorn berries until just before the first frost, allowing their flavour to fully develop, before adding them to the gin for a long period of infusion.

03 / From a Village
BAIJIA VILLAGE
CHANGTU / TIELING / CHINA

THE LABEL
IS THE ALBUM COVER.

The hawthorn berries were harvested from the distiller’s hometown in Tieling—Bai Jia Village, Tongjiangkou Township, Changtu County. A photograph of the village’s large pond after it had frozen over in winter was used for this bottle label (album cover), as a tribute to the small village where he grew up.
