Context /  Digital transformation background and rollout scope

Digital transformation / CMB context

Head-office system build and branch-level deployment.

In 2020, CMB’s head office accelerated the digitalisation of high-frequency corporate services. By April, system construction, platform setup and process re-engineering were completed, and the “new account journey” project was fully rolled out, enabling end-to-end online processing. Leveraging the head office’s established capabilities in document management, system upgrades, data quality, customer experience and risk-control integration, the Dalian branch carried out the system rollout across its 19 outlets, addressing system-to-branch process adaptation, frontline staff training, and local implementation of tiered risk-control standards.

Regional scope / rollout coverage

Covering 19 outlets in Dalian (incl. Jinzhou & Yingkou).

As the project lead, the business department set up a dedicated working group, defined responsibilities across units, conducted full-scale operational training, and piloted the system in batches before rolling it out to all outlets in the region.

○ Tiered Customer Due Diligence (CDD) framework /  Tag-based classification and differentiated review

Low-risk tags

Trusted and related customers

  • Trusted customers: credit customers, SOEs, joint-stock, listed, and multinational corporations
  • Related customers: same legal representative, BO, payment contact, or online-banking administrator
Medium-risk customers

Requiring supplementary verification

  • Annual reports and financial statements
  • Registered / paid-in capital of a certain scale
High-risk customers

Requiring enhanced measures

  • Individual businesses (sole proprietorships)
  • Non-operating entities
Medium-low risk / simplified review

Reducing pre-opening verification standards for medium-low risk customers

Appropriately streamline verification items to optimise the account-opening process and improve customer experience.

High-risk / enhanced review

Raising verification requirements during account opening for high-risk customers

  • On-site CDD visits
  • Business background checks (invoices, contracts, etc.)
  • Telephone verification of key corporate personnel
