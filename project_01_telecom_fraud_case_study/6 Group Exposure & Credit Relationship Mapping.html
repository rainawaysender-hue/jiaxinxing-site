CONTEXT

As the property sector and other high-risk industries placed increasing pressure on the real economy and related industries, credit risks began to emerge across interconnected corporate groups. In some cases, multiple companies with the same or closely related actual controllers had been managed as separate borrowers, resulting in fragmented exposure visibility and potential multiple-credit risks.

The challenge was not simply a lack of customer information, but the difficulty of connecting fragmented information across customers, systems and business processes to identify substantive relationships.

RELATIONSHIP IDENTIFICATION
From fragmented records to structured relationship evidence

I designed a Relationship Customer Query Tool that used the bank's internal customer ID as the primary reference point and performed cross-system matching across multiple relationship dimensions.

The mechanism did not simply return identical information.

Instead, matched relationships were differentiated according to their strength and evidential relevance.

STRONG Actual controller · Beneficial owner · Shareholder / controlling relationship · Common legal representative
Strongest indicators of a potential substantive relationship.
SUPPORTING Payment contact · Online banking administrator · Telephone number · Registered / business address
Supporting evidence that becomes more meaningful when combined with other signals.
SUPPLEMENTARY Shared counterparties · Similar transaction behaviour · Other overlapping business information
Supplementary evidence used to support further review rather than independently determine a relationship.
A DATA MATCH ≠ A CONCLUSION
A data match should generate evidence —
not automatically generate a conclusion.

CREDIT PROCESS

Relationship screening was embedded into the existing credit workflow while preserving clear separation between business initiation, credit assessment and group relationship management.

Relationship Manager BUSINESS INITIATION

Before submitting a credit-related application, the relationship manager conducts a relationship search and reviews identified customer connections. Confirmed substantive relationships are initiated for group relationship maintenance.

Credit Approval · Risk Management · Investment & Financing Functions CREDIT ASSESSMENT

Relationship information identified by the business team is reviewed alongside the credit application to ensure that group relationships are considered before credit exposure is established.

Group Management Function GROUP RELATIONSHIP MANAGEMENT

Confirmed relationships are incorporated into the internal group genealogy and used to support consolidated management of related corporate customers.

Group Management · Risk Management PERIODIC RE-SCREENING

Existing credit and investment customers are periodically screened in batches. Changes in ownership, key personnel, customer information or relationship structures trigger further review and maintenance of the group genealogy.

APPLICATION

After approximately one year of stable operation, the relationship-screening model developed by the Dalian Branch was reviewed and adopted as a reference by relevant teams at China Merchants Bank's headquarters.

The model provided a practical basis for further strengthening cross-regional group risk management, helping address information fragmentation between branches while supporting more consistent identification of corporate relationships.

KEY CONTRIBUTION
Making hidden relationships visible.
The core contribution was not simply the development of a query tool.
It was the transformation of fragmented customer information and experience-based relationship identification into a structured, reviewable process.
