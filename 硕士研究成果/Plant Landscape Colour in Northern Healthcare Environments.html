08 · Same Scene, Different Users /  Same environment ≠ same perception

Patients
Higher restorative requirements. More sensitive to overly intense red / yellow configurations.
Preferred: Dark Green
Family Members
More positive responses to brighter seasonal colours. Higher overall restorative evaluation.
Preferred: Red · Yellow
Healthcare Workers
Evaluation between the two groups. Accepted brighter colours but highest scores for dark-green-dominant scenes.
Preferred: Balanced
picture 8 (Deep Green > Red > Yellow) showed the smallest difference across the three user groups — the most universally acceptable configuration.
Research findings translated into planting principles.
01 · Base

Establish a green structural base

Use deep green as a stable visual foundation, especially where seasonal colour availability is limited.

02 · Proportion

Control accent intensity

Use red and yellow as measured accents rather than allowing high-intensity colours to dominate the scene.

03 · Season

Design for seasonal continuity

Shift the dominant colour strategy across spring, summer, autumn and winter rather than applying one fixed palette.

09 · From Research to Site /  Central Garden · First Affiliated Hospital

Harbin Medical University · Research-based site application
Site Analysis
Typology: Rule-based open space
Existing vegetation: No evergreen plants · No large canopy trees
Planting pattern: Scattered · Low ornamental value
Winter / early spring: Low visual interest
Summer: Insufficient shade
Design response: Introduce evergreen species · Add canopy trees · Create layered planting · Control colour proportions based on research findings

10 · Site Strategy /  Seasonal colour continuity through plant selection

Each colour role defined by its seasonal transition
Spring Light Green / Deep Green
Summer Light Green / Deep Green
Autumn Deep Green > Red > Yellow
Winter Deep Green / Evergreen Structure
Selection logic: Main tone — spring light green, autumn deep green (70%). Auxiliary tone — spring light green, autumn red (20%). Accent tone — spring light green, autumn yellow (10%). All selected species follow evidence-based seasonal colour transitions.
